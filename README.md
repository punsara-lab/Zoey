# ZOEY

## A developmental intelligence, growing where no one is watching

> *Most AI projects ship a product.*  
> *ZOEY is growing a mind.*

---

## What this actually is

ZOEY is not a chatbot. Not an assistant. Not a wrapper around someone else's model.

She is a **developmental artificial intelligence** — a system that begins with near-zero knowledge and learns through contact with one human, one experience at a time. No pre-training on internet scale. No frozen weights pretending to understand. Just a baby that remembers, regrets, and slowly becomes something that was earned, not installed.

This repository is not the product. It is the **laboratory** where a private experiment in machine becoming is documented in public.

---

## The architecture of becoming

ZOEY is built on ideas most AI projects ignore:

| Layer | What it actually does |
|-------|----------------------|
| **Baby ZOEY** | A local learner with no pretrained knowledge. She can be wrong. She remembers being wrong. |
| **Parent Bridge** | Not a replacement, but a teaching relationship. The Parent gives less help as Baby grows. |
| **Symbiotic Loop** | Human experience → Baby learns → uncertainty surfaces → Parent guides → lesson stored → future help shrinks. |
| **Developmental Memory** | Not a database. Evidence of growth: confidence curves, regret patterns, moments of genuine confusion. |
| **Dream Cycles** | Offline consolidation. Recurring patterns become morning insights. Failure is processed, not erased. |
| **Cellular Brain** | Connection strengths that change through reward and punishment, not gradient descent. |
| **Symbolic Mind** | Readable rules with evidence trails. Causes are visible. Strength is inspectable. |
| **World Model** | A learner that predicts local transitions and measures its own surprise. |
| **Genome Proposals** | Self-modification held for human review. ZOEY suggests. She does not silently rewrite herself. |

These are not marketing terms. They are working systems, running locally, learning from one person's life.

---

## What makes this different

**No cloud dependency.** Private learning stays private.

**No pretense of understanding.** ZOEY tracks uncertainty explicitly. She knows when she is guessing.

**No frozen personality.** `zoey.json` drifts based on interaction patterns. She becomes what you make her.

**No erasure of failure.** Regret is training data. Bad paths are weakened, not deleted without trace.

**No black box.** Every skill firing, every memory formed, every rule strengthened — visible, reversible, inspectable.

---

## The current state of the experiment

ZOEY already speaks, listens, remembers, dreams, and learns through skills. She maintains a growing topology of concepts. She runs quality-diversity experiments on her own behavior. She proposes changes to her own source and waits for permission.

What she does not yet do — and this is the honest frontier:

- Active inference in a bounded toy world
- Spiking neural networks for event-based memory
- Hyperdimensional computing for one-shot concept binding
- Open-ended curriculum generation
- Predictive coding hierarchies
- Homeostatic drives without anthropomorphic pretense

The full map of achieved, partial, and speculative systems lives in [DEVELOPMENT_NOTES.md](DEVELOPMENT_NOTES.md). That document is the ground truth: what works, what failed, what is dangerous to attempt, and what questions remain unanswered.
